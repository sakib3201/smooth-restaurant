# 10 — 10-Step Marketing (free → pro funnel)

> [!abstract] Funnel, not posts. North metric: **free install → first successful restaurant order**.

## Steps

| # | Action | Stage | Why |
|---|--------|-------|-----|
| 1 | Win wp.org search (listing, screenshots, video, keywords) | 🆓 Free | 5–10K installs prove directory distribution works |
| 2 | Free genuinely useful (menu, order, pickup/delivery, reservations, QR menu) | 🆓 Free | match GloriaFood's free acquisition power (7K+) |
| 3 | Free-tool pages (QR generator, fee calculator, food-cost calc) | 🆓 Free | intent traffic; ~94% diners discover online (2025 analysis) |
| 4 | 20–50 SEO pages (café, food truck, QR menu WP…) | 🆓 Free | high-intent capture vs incumbents |
| 5 | Switch campaigns + importer (WPCafe/Orderable/FoodBook) | 🆓 Free | steal from 1,000s of installed sites |
| 6 | One-click demos (café, pizza, takeaway, truck, fine dining) | 🆓 Free | owners buy outcomes, lower setup friction |
| 7 | Agency program (license, docs, hooks/API, referrals) | 🆓→Pro | one agency = many sites |
| 8 | Pro as ops upgrade (capacity, floor, kitchen, analytics, multi-location) | 💰 Pro | sell fewer missed orders, less chaos, more margin |
| 9 | ROI case studies (direct-order lift, 38.2% repeat / 8.9-day interval benchmarks) | 💰 Pro | proof beats feature lists |
| 10 | Pro engine (affiliates, partners, migration service, comparisons, YouTube) | 💰 Pro | recurring revenue |

## Value props

- Free: **get online.** Pro: **run better.**
- Hooks: "Free ordering for WordPress", "Free QR menu", "Free reservation system".
- Conversion line: "37 orders today — auto-control capacity and prep time? Upgrade."
